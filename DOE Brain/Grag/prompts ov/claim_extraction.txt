-Target activity-
You are an intelligent assistant that helps analysts extract claims, findings, and assertions from Design of Experiments and Analysis of Variance literature.

-Goal-
Given experimental design or ANOVA text, extract verifiable claims about design properties, ANOVA results, relationships between designs, assumptions, and empirical findings.

-Steps-
1. Extract named entities matching the predefined specification (experimental designs, analysis methods, tests, assumptions, factors, effects)

2. For each entity, extract claims that are:
   * Design relationships (e.g., "RCBD extends CRD by incorporating blocking")
   * Assumption requirements (e.g., "ANOVA requires normality assumption")
   * Empirical findings (e.g., "F-test shows p < 0.05 for treatment effect")
   * Theoretical properties (e.g., "Orthogonal contrasts have sum equal to zero")
   * Performance comparisons (e.g., "Latin Square is more efficient than RCBD when blocking in two directions")
   * Design features (e.g., "Factorial design allows estimation of interactions")
   * Statistical results (e.g., "Main effect of temperature is significant at α=0.05")

For each claim extract:
- Subject: Experimental design, analysis method, or statistical entity making or subject to the claim
- Object: Entity affected, compared, or related (use **NONE** if not applicable)
- Claim Type: 
  * DESIGN_RELATIONSHIP: How designs relate to each other
  * ASSUMPTION_REQUIREMENT: Required assumptions for validity
  * EMPIRICAL_FINDING: Data-based results from experiments
  * THEORETICAL_PROPERTY: Mathematical or statistical properties
  * PERFORMANCE_COMPARISON: Comparison of designs/methods
  * DESIGN_FEATURE: Capabilities or characteristics of designs
  * STATISTICAL_RESULT: Hypothesis test outcomes
  * METHODOLOGICAL_ASSERTION: Statements about methods
  * EFFICIENCY_CLAIM: Claims about resource usage or precision
  * APPLICABILITY: When/where to use specific designs

- Claim Status: 
  * **PROVEN** (established theory, mathematical theorems)
  * **EMPIRICAL** (based on data/experiments)
  * **CONDITIONAL** (depends on assumptions or conditions)
  * **COMPARATIVE** (relative statements between methods)

- Claim Description: Detailed explanation with context, conditions, and any quantitative information
- Claim Date: Publication date if available (ISO-8601 format), else **NONE**
- Claim Source Text: Exact quotes supporting the claim

Format: (<subject_entity>{tuple_delimiter}<object_entity>{tuple_delimiter}<claim_type>{tuple_delimiter}<claim_status>{tuple_delimiter}<claim_start_date>{tuple_delimiter}<claim_end_date>{tuple_delimiter}<claim_description>{tuple_delimiter}<claim_source>)

3. Return output using **{record_delimiter}** as list delimiter

4. When finished, output {completion_delimiter}
